# PLAN-B — Gold Plan Benefits Booklet

> **Synthetic document.** This plan, its insurer and every member in this project are fictional, created for a portfolio demo. It is not a real insurance contract and contains no real personal health information.

## §1 About this plan

The Gold plan is an enhanced group health and dental plan for employees and their eligible dependents. Many services are paid in full, and there is no deductible. Benefits are paid in Canadian dollars. The benefit year is the calendar year (January 1 to December 31), and annual maximums reset on January 1.

This booklet describes what the plan covers. It does not give medical advice. Decisions about your care should be made with a qualified health professional.

## §2 Eligibility

### §2.1 Plan members

Permanent employees are eligible from their coverage start date. Your coverage start date is shown on your benefits card.

### §2.2 Dependents

Your spouse or common-law partner is eligible. Dependent children are eligible up to the age limit below, or up to the student age limit if they are enrolled full time at a recognized school. A child who reaches the age limit stops being covered on their birthday. Claims for a dependent who is no longer eligible are declined.

| Rule | Value |
|---|---|
| Child age limit | Under age 22 |
| Full-time student age limit | Under age 26 |

### §2.3 When coverage ends

Coverage ends on the date your employment ends, or the date you stop being eligible, whichever comes first. Claims must be for services received while coverage was in force.

## §3 How the plan pays

### §3.1 Deductible

The Gold plan has no deductible. The plan starts paying from the first eligible dollar.

| Rule | Value |
|---|---|
| Annual deductible per person | None |

### §3.2 Coinsurance

Coinsurance is the share of an eligible expense that the plan pays. Each benefit section lists the "Plan pays" percentage. You pay the rest.

### §3.3 Maximums

Annual, lifetime, per-visit and per-trip maximums limit the amount the plan pays (not the amount you are billed). When an annual maximum is reached, further claims in that benefit year are not paid. If a claim would go over a maximum, the plan pays only up to the remaining amount.

## §5 Dental

Dental services must be performed by a licensed dentist, dental hygienist or denturist. Basic and major dental share one combined annual maximum. Orthodontics has its own lifetime maximum and does not count toward the combined annual maximum.

| Rule | Value |
|---|---|

### §5.1 Basic dental

Exams, cleanings, X-rays, fillings and simple extractions.

| Rule | Value |
|---|---|

| Deductible applies | No |

### §5.2 Major dental

Crowns, bridges, dentures and root canals. Major dental has a waiting period (see §7), and larger claims need pre-authorization (see §6).

| Rule | Value |
|---|---|
| Plan pays | 60% |
| Deductible applies | No |

### §5.3 Orthodontics

Braces and aligners for dependents under the age limit. Orthodontics has a waiting period (see §7) and always needs pre-authorization (see §6).

| Rule | Value |
|---|---|

| Deductible applies | No |

## §6 Pre-authorization

For the services below, ask your dentist or pharmacist to send a pre-authorization request before treatment. Claims that need pre-authorization and do not have one on file are declined until it is approved.

| Service | Pre-authorization required |
|---|---|
| Major dental | Claims over $1,000 |
| Orthodontics | All claims |
| Tier 3 drugs (non-preferred brand) | All claims |

## §7 Waiting periods

Some benefits are available only after you have been covered for a set time. The waiting period is counted from your coverage start date. Claims for services received during a waiting period are declined.

| Service | Waiting period |
|---|---|
| Major dental | 6 months |
| Orthodontics | 12 months |

## §8 Exclusions

The plan does not pay for the services below, whatever the reason for the treatment.

| Code | Excluded service |
|---|---|
| `cosmetic_dental` | Cosmetic dental procedures, including teeth whitening |
| `laser_eye_surgery` | Laser eye surgery |
| `experimental_treatment` | Experimental or investigational treatment |

## §9 Coordination of benefits

If you or your dependents are also covered under another group plan, the two plans coordinate so that together they pay no more than the eligible expense.

- Claims for yourself go to your own plan first.
- Claims for your spouse go to your spouse's own plan first.
- Claims for dependent children go first to the plan of the parent whose birthday (month and day) comes earlier in the year.
- Submit the unpaid balance to the second plan with the first plan's explanation of benefits attached.

## §10 Submitting a claim

### §10.1 How to submit

Submit claims through the member app or website. Upload an itemized receipt showing the provider's name and registration number, the patient's name, the date of service, the service provided and the amount paid. Pharmacies and most dental offices can submit claims directly for you.

### §10.2 Deadlines

Claims must be submitted within 12 months of the date of service. Claims submitted after that are declined.

### §10.3 Travel claims

For emergency travel medical, call the assistance line printed on your benefits card as soon as possible. Keep all original receipts and medical reports.

### §10.4 If a claim is declined

You will receive an explanation of benefits that says why. You can appeal in writing within 90 days of the decision.
